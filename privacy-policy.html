ChessRivals Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

ChessRivals is a chess game for iPhone. You can play against computer opponents, play other people online, join clans, chat with clanmates, open chests and earn coins. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect only what the game needs to work, we do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who is responsible for your information

The app is run by Luke Woolley, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your information. You can contact us at lukewoolley.uk@gmail.com.

Information we collect

WhatWhen and why
Email addressWhen you sign up or sign in with email, Apple or Google. Used to create and secure your account and to contact you about it. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's private relay address.
PasswordIf you sign up with email and password. It is handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form. We never see it in readable form.
Username and profileThe name you choose, your Elo rating, badges, equipped items and clan. Other players can see these.
Profile pictureOptional. Only if you choose to take or upload a picture. The app asks permission for the camera or your photo library at that moment, and only reads the picture you pick.
Gameplay informationYour games, moves, results, rating changes, missions, daily rewards, coins and the items you own, so your progress is saved and matches can be played fairly.
Friends, clans and messagesFriend lists, clan membership, clan chat messages, coin requests and challenges. Clan messages are visible to the members of that clan.
Online statusWhether you are online or in a match, so friends and opponents can see it and matchmaking works.
Purchase informationWhen you buy coins, Apple processes the payment. We receive confirmation of what was bought and a purchase identifier, linked to your account. We never receive your card or Apple Account payment details.
Reports you sendIf you report a player, message, picture or clan, we receive your report, the category and optional explanation, and the reported content needed to review it.
Technical informationOur service providers may log basic technical data such as IP address and request times for security and reliability.

The app also keeps your settings and some progress on your device so screens load quickly. This stays on your device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, health data, or browsing history.

How we use your information

  • To run the game: accounts, matchmaking, ratings, clans, chat, rewards and purchases.
  • To keep players safe: moderating chat and names, handling reports, and banning accounts that break the rules.
  • To prevent fraud and cheating, and to keep the service secure.
  • To contact you about your account or to respond when you write to us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Under UK GDPR our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running the game you signed up for), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, moderation and improving the game), your consent (camera and photo access, and your profile picture), and legal obligations.

Some opponents in the game are computer-controlled. No personal information is shared with them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app, and they may process your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
  • RevenueCat: checks and records in-app purchases.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, the App Store and in-app purchase processing.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend: sends us the e-mail when a report is submitted.

Other players can see your username, profile picture, rating, badges, clan and online status. Clanmates can see your clan chat messages. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of players or the service.

Where your information is processed

Our providers may process information outside the United Kingdom, including in the European Economic Area and the United States. Where that happens we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the provider's standard contractual terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except where we must keep something for legal reasons or to prevent abuse, such as a record that an account was banned. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings > Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple Account at the same time. If you cannot use the app, e-mail us and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us at lukewoolley.uk@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are in the UK and are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

ChessRivals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted by account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app.
