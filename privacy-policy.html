Chess Royale Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Chess Royale ("the app", "we") is operated by Luke Woolley, United Kingdom. Contact: lukewoolley.uk@gmail.com. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our hosting provider, Supabase), or, if you use "Continue with Apple", the identifier and email (which may be Apple's private relay address) Apple gives us.
  • Profile: the username you choose, and an optional profile picture. Your username, Elo rating and profile picture are visible to other players, for example in friend lists, clans and challenges.
  • Game data: your Elo, games played and results, game history, missions, badges, gems, items and cosmetics you own, settings and your friends and clan membership.
  • Purchases: if you buy gems, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We and our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receive a record of the purchase (product, transaction ID and your account ID). We never see your card details.
  • Safety reports: if you report or block a player or clan, we store the report, the reason and who made it.
  • Technical data: limited technical information (such as IP address and device type) is processed by our hosting providers to run and secure the service.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics tools. Chess analysis and bot opponents run on your device.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, run games and matchmaking with friends, keep your progress, show your name and rating to other players, process purchases, prevent cheating and abuse, handle reports, provide support and comply with the law.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase (hosting, database and sign-in).
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google (purchases).
  • Other players: your username, rating and profile picture.
  • Authorities, where legally required.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account), your account, profile, progress, items, friends, clan membership and game history are permanently deleted from our servers. Reports about other players, and purchase records we must keep for accounting or legal reasons, may be kept longer.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account in the app at any time. You can also block and report other players. Depending on where you live (including the UK and EU), you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). Email us at lukewoolley.uk@gmail.com to use these rights.

Children

Chess Royale is not intended for children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and transfers

We use industry-standard protections, but no service is perfectly secure. Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, using safeguards required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
